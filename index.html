PanelFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

PanelFlip is a comic and book reader. Your library data, reading progress, settings, and imported files are stored locally on your device.

PanelFlip does not collect personal information for the developer. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry, and it does not require an account.

Optional cover and metadata lookups

When you intentionally perform a cover or metadata lookup, PanelFlip sends the search term you enter—for example, “Batman Year One”—to the comic metadata API you selected.

The selected third-party API returns cover images and publication or book metadata. That service may receive normal network information, such as your IP address, and is governed by its own privacy policy.

PanelFlip does not operate a server that receives or stores these lookup requests.

Your data

You can remove PanelFlip's local app data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.
